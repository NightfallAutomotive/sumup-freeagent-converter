Uploading and explaining in FreeAgent

In FreeAgent, add a bank account called "SumUp" and upload this file there, not to your main bank account. Then explain each kind of line like this:

SaleSales income, or match it to the invoice it paid.
FeeBank, credit card and finance charges.
Held backRepayment of your SumUp cash advance or loan. Check with your accountant if you're unsure what it is.
To bankTransfer to your main bank account. There's one line per payout, so it matches the deposit on your bank statement.
RefundRefund or chargeback against the original sale.

Independent tool. Not made by or affiliated with SumUp or FreeAgent. Check the totals against your SumUp payouts before reconciling, and ask your accountant if you're unsure how to explain a line.
